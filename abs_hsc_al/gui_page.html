ABS HSC Rules (July 2022) – aluminum scantling checker

Design history (project)

Save the design as a numbered revision at each design phase. Each revision keeps the full inputs and its check results, so you can reopen it, keep editing, and compare how the structure stays within the Rules as the design evolves. A project is one .hscproj.json file you keep; a working draft is also kept automatically in this browser.

Project information (for the printed report)

Stations (pressures along the length)

Stations run from the aft perpendicular (x/L = 0) to the forward perpendicular (x/L = 1). Enter the deadrise at each section and read Kv (Fig. 4) and, for craft under 61 m, Fv (Fig. 5) from the Rule figures. Panels and stiffeners can then be tied to a station by name. Leave empty to skip.

Aluminum material

Take the four strengths (N/mm²) for your alloy/temper from ABS Part 2, Ch.5, Sec.5 (2-5-5). The library below fills them from 2-5-5 (unwelded) and 2-4-5 Table 2 (as-welded); you can still type your own. The example file's values are illustrative only.

Plating

Each load case is checked for thickness; secondary-stiffening and minimum thickness are added automatically.

Stiffeners and girders

Hull girder (midship section)

Uses the midship section calculated above. Type values here only to override them (all three are needed); leave all blank and without a section to skip the hull girder check.

Hull girder shear (3-2-1/1.9)

Required for craft of 61 m and over (for twin-hull craft under 61 m only with unusual or concentrated loads). For each station enter the Rule distribution factors F1 and F2 (read from 3-2-1/1.9.2 Figures 3 and 4) and, if you have it, the still-water shear force Fsw. If you leave Fsw empty it is taken as zero and the report says so. The first moment m and the side-shell thickness come from the midship section above unless you enter them here.

Multi-hull cross structure (3-2-1/3.3, 3.5, 3-2-A2)

For vessels with two hulls (set number of hulls = 2 and the hull centreline spacing above). The program lists Mtb, Mtt and Qt and checks the cross structure when you enter its offered transverse section modulus SMt (cm²-m) and shear area At (cm²). Add the connecting elements (frames or beams) to distribute the pitch connecting moment Mtt by 3-2-A2 and check each element in bending and shear.
